## Demonstration inventory

The repeatable seed contains ten fictional scenarios, including official challan procedure, ambiguous FIR/indirect phrasing, receipt context, gesture-only ambiguity, unofficial-payment concern, coercion and officer/public safety exercises. Speaker labels in these records are authored roles, not inferred identities. Playback is a generated silent title card; it is not a video reenactment of the transcript. Notes, events, review history and artifacts exist as database records and can be exercised through the application.

The optional OpenRouter reading guide now performs actual excerpt selection on authored synthetic scenarios, after reviewer preview and consent. API/UI tests and one live saved-case flow verify source references, privacy boundaries and persistence. This adds a navigation aid, not scenario-recognition accuracy, learned contextual intent, RAG or free-form factual generation. It does not change any scenario coverage claim above.

Local anonymous diarization and multilingual vector retrieval are also implemented and tested with synthetic fixtures. They add uncertain speaker turns and searchable exact excerpts; neither recognizes identity, legal meaning or intent. The retrieval layer does not generate answers or complete a generative RAG system. These features do not change the scenario-recognition claims above.

The PRD asks for at least 50 scenario evaluations and quality metrics. **That deliverable is not complete.** There are ten demo cases and a larger technical test suite with parameterized examples. These counts must not be described as 50 consented video scenarios or used to fabricate precision, recall, accuracy or F1. The [evaluation harness and 50-slot collection plan](EVALUATION.md) are implemented, now including speaker error and independent reviewer agreement calculations. The plan contains no recordings or invented reference labels. Follow its source-separated annotation procedure to complete the research component.
